    This browser does not let a web page set the volume (every iPhone), so it cannot crossfade.

    This browser can keep the screen on only for a secure address: open SoundStorm at its https address to use it.

    Lower qualities use less data and start faster on a slow connection. Downloads always keep the original.     Reach it from anywhere

    Use SoundStorm away from home with the same login. No app or extra account needed.

    This puts your server on the internet: anyone with the address reaches your login screen.

    To set it up, open Set up Tailscale from the Start menu on the computer SoundStorm runs on, or read how.

    Rather not be on the internet? Tailscale reaches it privately from your own devices instead. It needs a free Tailscale account and its app on each device.

    Scrobbling

    Keep a lifelong record of what you listen to on ListenBrainz, free and open, with statistics and recommendations. Every song you play here, on any device, is sent to your own account there.

    Sign in to ListenBrainz, open your settings, copy your User token and paste it here.

    Lyrics

    Songs with lyrics in their files always show those. For the rest, SoundStorm can look them up on LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, the first time each song plays. Its lyrics are added by its community of contributors, and Now Playing says so under any it supplied.

    Sends the artist, title and album of the song being played to LRCLIB. Nothing else leaves your server, and the lyrics are kept by SoundStorm, not added to your music folders. To do a whole library at once, try LRCGET.

    Music discovery

    Artist pages can show a short bio and the artists like them that you already have, and artist mixes can play those artists too. SoundStorm finds out from MusicBrainz, ListenBrainz and Wikipedia - free and open, no account needed. Artist bios are Wikipedia's text, shared under CC BY-SA and credited under each one.

    Sends an artist's name when you open their page or play a mix of them. Nothing else leaves your server, and each artist is asked about once - the answers are kept for months.

    Read-along

    When a book is on the shelf as both an ebook and an audiobook, SoundStorm can line the two up so the page turns with the audio and the sentence being read is highlighted.

    Each book is listened to once, in the background, one at a time - about 40 minutes for a 10-hour book on a recent computer. Turned off, you can still sync a book from Books, Read Along.

    People

    Everyone gets their own login and their own place in every book. Tick the shelves each person can see. With Pictures, each person sees only their own photos (you see everyone's), up to their photo space.

      Your photos

      From Google Photos

      1. Open Google Takeout and sign in. Only Google Photos is ticked.
      2. Choose Export once, .zip, and the largest size (50 GB), then Create export.
      3. Google emails you when it is ready - hours, or a day or two for a big library. Download every zip it gives you.

      From iCloud (iPhone)

      1. Open Apple's data and privacy page, sign in, and choose Request a copy of your data.
      2. Tick iCloud Photos, choose the largest size, and send the request.
      3. Apple emails you when it is ready - usually within a week. Download every zip.

      Then drop the zips anywhere on this window, or choose them below. Dates and places are kept, each photo goes into your folder by when it was taken, and photos you already have here are skipped. Large downloads carry on where they stopped if the connection drops; keep this page open while they send.
