    Settings

    Add media

    Music, films, TV, audiobooks, ebooks, documents or photos. SoundStorm puts each one on the right shelf. You can also drag files or whole folders anywhere onto the SoundStorm window.

    Check for new files is for anything copied into the library folder some other way. Files added here appear by themselves.

    Playback on this device

    This browser does not let a web page set the volume (every iPhone), so it cannot crossfade.

    Lower qualities use less data and start faster on a slow connection. Downloads always keep the original. Crossfade leaves an album that is playing in order alone, so it still flows as the album does. "Lower on mobile data" works on Android; an iPhone cannot tell its browser which connection it is on.

    Use on your phone or TV

    Open this in the browser on a phone, tablet, TV or another computer on the same Wi-Fi, and sign in with the same account.

    Reach it from anywhere

    Use SoundStorm away from home with the same login. No app or extra account needed.

    This puts your server on the internet: anyone with the address reaches your login screen.

    To set it up, open Set up Tailscale from the Start menu on the computer SoundStorm runs on, or read how.

    Rather not be on the internet? Tailscale reaches it privately from your own devices instead. It needs a free Tailscale account and its app on each device.

    Lyrics

    Songs with lyrics in their files always show those. For the rest, SoundStorm can look them up on LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, the first time each song plays.

    Sends the artist, title and album of the song being played to LRCLIB. Nothing else leaves your server, and the lyrics are kept by SoundStorm, not added to your music folders. To do a whole library at once, try LRCGET.

    Read-along

    When a book is on the shelf as both an ebook and an audiobook, SoundStorm can line the two up so the page turns with the audio and the sentence being read is highlighted.

    Each book is listened to once, in the background, one at a time - about 40 minutes for a 10-hour book on a recent computer. Turned off, you can still sync a book from Books, Read & listen.

    People

    Everyone gets their own login and their own place in every book. Tick the shelves each person can see.
