    Reach it from anywhere

    Use SoundStorm away from home with the same login. No app or extra account needed.

    This puts your server on the internet: anyone with the address reaches your login screen.

    To set it up, open Set up Tailscale from the Start menu on the computer SoundStorm runs on, or read how.

    Rather not be on the internet? Tailscale reaches it privately from your own devices instead. It needs a free Tailscale account and its app on each device.

    Lyrics

    Songs with lyrics in their files always show those. For the rest, SoundStorm can look them up on LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, the first time each song plays.

    Sends the artist, title and album of the song being played to LRCLIB. Nothing else leaves your server, and the lyrics are kept by SoundStorm, not added to your music folders. To do a whole library at once, try LRCGET.

    People

    Everyone gets their own login and their own place in every book. Tick the shelves each person can see.
