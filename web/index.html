Your own private cloud, at home.

One app for your family's photos, films, TV, music, audiobooks and books, on your phones and TVs. It runs on a computer in your house, so your memories stay yours. Free, open source, and no subscriptions.

Everything in one app

Not a server to manage: one sign-in, one search, one app your whole family just uses.

Photos

Your phones back up by themselves. A timeline of months and days, people and places, videos and Live Photos. Bring your library in from Google or Apple, and send photos to each other.

Films & TV

Shows season by season, carry on where you stopped, subtitles and audio languages, Up next. Start a film on your phone, watch it on the TV, and control it from your phone.

Music

Albums, artists, playlists, radio and moods, synced lyrics, and animations that move with every beat. Bring your playlists from Plex, or any player that saves M3U files.

Audiobooks & books

Chapters, speed and a sleep timer. Read along as the page turns with the narrator, or read alone at your own pace. Turn an ebook into an audiobook, or an audiobook into an ebook, on your own computer.

For the whole family

Everyone gets their own sign-in, favorites, places in books and their own private photos. Invite family with a QR code.

At home and away

Works on your Wi-Fi from the first minute, with a secure address of its own. Away from home too, once you turn it on and where your internet connection allows it. No account with anyone.

Yours, and private

Your media stays in your house

Your photos, films, music, searches and passwords stay on your own computer, and EmberStorm sends no usage data. It goes online for its secure address, for updates when you run them, and for things you switch on; the media servers inside it also fetch film posters and download their own models. Exactly what, and why.

Free and open source

Every line is public, under the GNU AGPL, so anyone can check what it does. No subscriptions, no ads, no account to make.

Install it

About 10 to 30 minutes, mostly downloading. You need a computer that stays on, ideally with 16 GB of memory, and about 20 GB of disk for EmberStorm itself, plus your media.

  1. Download EmberStorm for Windows
  2. Right-click the downloaded file → Properties → tick Unblock → OK. Windows blocks every downloaded script until you say you trust it.
  3. Double-click it. A setup window walks you through the rest, and opens EmberStorm when it's done.
  1. Run this in a terminal:
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GabrielHollberg/emberstorm/main/install.sh | sh
  2. It sets up Docker if needed (your computer asks for your password once), then everything else, and prints the address to open. On a Mac, a Docker window may open the first time: click Skip, no account needed.

Then get the apps

  • Android, Google TV and Fire TV: from the releases page (the android ones). They find your server by themselves.
  • iPhone and Apple TV: in testing, coming to the App Store. Until then an iPhone uses it in Safari.
  • Any phone or computer: works in the web browser too, and adds to the home screen.

Or get the EmberStorm box

A small, quiet box with EmberStorm already on it. Plug it into power and your router, scan the code on the sticker with your phone, and make your account. Nothing to install, nothing to configure.
